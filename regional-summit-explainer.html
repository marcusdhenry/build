D1 vs D2

Program Size, Not Skill

This is about the size of the gym program, not how good the team is. It keeps competition fair -- like weight classes in boxing.

How Scoring Works

The Floating Score System

Cyrene's team performs Saturday and Sunday. The system rewards the better performance more heavily, so one rough round doesn't sink the weekend.

Example -- Say Cyrene's Team Scores:
Saturday (Round 1) 92.5
Sunday (Round 2) -- better day 96.0
Final Score 96.0 × 0.75 + 92.5 × 0.25 = 95.125
💡
The "floating" part: It doesn't matter which day is better. Whichever round scores higher automatically becomes the 75%. The team has two chances to peak.
Bids Explained

How Teams Get Invited

A "bid" is an invitation to compete. Cyrene's team earned one during the regular season. There are three tiers -- all get you in, but they differ in how much registration is covered.

🎟️

At-Large Bid

Top 3 at any qualifying event earn one. Most common way in.

$0 off registration
🎫

Partial-Paid Bid

Covers 25% per athlete. Stack up to 4 for a full ride.

25% off per athlete
🏆

Full Paid Bid

Covers entire registration. Awarded at major nationals only.

100% covered

Know Before You Go

Venue Rules & Tips

Key rules for the Georgia International Convention Center.

🎟️

Digital Tickets Only

No physical tickets or cash at the door. Buy online with email + credit card. Scannable digital ticket.

🚫

No Outside Food

No food or drinks in. Concessions inside. Athletes can bring water bottles -- filling stations available.

📱

Recording Allowed

Personal recording on phones is fine. No commercial recording or live streaming from the venue.

🪑

Bring a Portable Chair

Venue seating fills up. You'll be there for hours between performances. Your chair is your base camp.

🅿️

Parking: $18/day

Self-parking at the venue. Rideshare from the Fairfield Inn is also an option (~5 min drive).

📺

Watch Live on Varsity TV

Share the stream link with family back home so they can watch Cyrene perform in real time.
