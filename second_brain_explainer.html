The Concept

What is a Second Brain?

A Second Brain is an external digital system that captures, organizes, and connects everything you learn, create, and want to remember — so your biological brain can focus on thinking, not storing.

Your Biological Brain

Extraordinary at making connections, generating ideas, and creative thinking — but terrible at storage. It forgets, conflates, and loses the brilliant idea you had at 2am.

⇄

Your Digital Second Brain

Perfect memory. Infinite capacity. Instantly searchable. Survives sleep, stress, and time. Lets your biological brain do what it's actually great at.

📥

Capture

Collect ideas, articles, meeting notes, research, and insights from everywhere — before they evaporate. Inoreader → Readwise → Craft is Marcus's pipeline.

🗂️

Organize

File information by where it will be useful, not where it came from. The PARA method keeps everything findable without taxing your memory.

🔗

Distill

Compress and connect ideas over time. The best insights from a book or conversation get surfaced and linked to active work when they're relevant.

🚀

Express

Build and create from what you've captured. Essays, briefs, presentations, and plans flow out of a well-maintained Second Brain instead of being created from scratch each time.

The Framework

PARA — How It's All Organized

Developed by Tiago Forte, PARA organizes everything by actionability — not by topic. Four buckets hold your entire digital life.

P
Projects

Active Work

Finite goals with a deadline. Things you're actively working toward right now.

A
Areas

Ongoing Roles

Responsibilities with no end date — things you maintain over time.

7 areas
R
Resources

Reference Material

Topics you're interested in that might be useful someday. Pure reference.

8 folders
A
Archive

Completed Work

Inactive items from the other buckets. Kept for reference. Out of the way.

367+ docs

The key insight: You don't file information by category (e.g., "health," "writing") — you file it by where it will be useful. A note about nutrition goes under a Project if you're actively working on weight loss, an Area if it's ongoing health maintenance, or Resources if it's just interesting research. Same information, different home depending on what's actionable for you right now.

The Journey

How the System Evolved

From scattered notes to a unified, MCP-powered knowledge system.

Pre-2024 · Background
Manual Era — Multiple Disconnected Tools
Notes scattered across various apps. No unified PARA structure. Ideas, projects, and reference material all in separate silos with no reliable retrieval system.
2024 · First System
Obsidian + the Four-Tool Stack
Built a PARA vault in Obsidian with numeric-prefixed folders. Required Syncthing for sync, Tailscale for secure network access, and TickTick as a separate task manager — four tools doing jobs that should be one.
Early 2025 · Capture Layer
Three-Tool Knowledge Pipeline
Established Inoreader (RSS triage) → Raindrop (passive bookmarking) → Readwise (intentional capture). Information from the world now flows into the system reliably instead of disappearing.
2025 · AI Integration
MCP Connections + Claude as Primary AI
Set up MCP integrations for Google Calendar, Gmail, Craft, and Readwise. Switched from ChatGPT to Claude. Built VoicePal voice-to-article workflow and an Apple Shortcuts voice-to-field-note system via the Claude API.
March 25, 2026 · Migration Complete
Craft Becomes the Single Source of Truth
Migrated from Obsidian to Craft. Four tools collapsed to one. Cloud-native (no Syncthing/Tailscale needed), daily notes built in, tasks as native checkboxes. All 8 active VA projects, home documentation, Notion databases, and personal knowledge moved over in one session.
