Recommended starting point

The 1990 Pro has a known treble peak around 8 kHz that can get fatiguing on bright recordings. Small adjustments tame that edge without losing the analytical character these headphones are known for.

Step by Step

How to actually set it

One press of Volume + or Volume − equals roughly 1 dB in that direction. Count your presses, not the exact shade of the volume buttons.

1
Press Menu once — Menu button lights Blue (Bass)
2
Press Volume + once — adds +1 dB bass
3
Press Menu twice more — skip Mid, land on Orange (Treble)
4
Press Volume − once — cuts −1 dB treble
5
Press Menu until Menu button goes dark — you're back to normal volume control
6
Listen for about a week before adjusting further. Your ears will tell you what to tweak.
Cheat Sheet

Quick reference

Crossfeed
Hold Menu for 2 seconds. Four levels. Try level 1 on older rock/jazz to reduce hard-panned stereo fatigue.
Line-level
Hold both volume buttons to lock for preamp/amp output. Not needed for headphones.
Desktop mode
Plug in charger at full battery — bypasses battery, preserves lifespan during long sessions.
Charging
Red = charging, blue/green = full. Use 5V/2A USB-C. Slow charge at 1A extends battery life.
Micro-USB
Audio data input. Handle gently — it's the one known weak point on the Mojo 2.
USB-C port
Charging only on most Mojo 2 units. Audio goes through Micro-USB, optical, or coax.
DSP memory
Settings persist through power off. Set once and forget.
